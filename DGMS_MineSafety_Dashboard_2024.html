Executive Summary | In 2024, coal mines recorded 38 fatal accidents with 49 fatalities — fatality rate rose to 0.14 per 1,000 employed (up from 0.12 in 2023). Transportation machinery (31%) and Falls (24%) are the leading causes. Non-coal mines saw 33 fatal accidents (up 18% YoY). Output per manshift hit a record 9.51 in 2024. Historically, fatality rates have fallen from 0.94 (1901-10) to 0.12 (2021-24) — an 87% reduction over 120 years.
